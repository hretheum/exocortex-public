# F3.1. Choosing a public corpus

[← F3](../F3-first-pass.md)

## Why

The result of an experiment can be checked only if anyone can download the same documents. The corpus has to be public, shareable, and fit the research question: many sentences about plans, hypotheses and goals next to sentences about facts. The starting point is the arXiv papers Exocortex already has: the ids are in the headers of the `wiki/papers` pages, and the engine's summaries are in the database.

## What to do

1. List the arXiv ids of all papers in `wiki/papers`, with the download date and the relevance score the engine gave them.
2. Establish the basis for use. arXiv makes paper metadata, including abstracts, available under CC0, which has to be confirmed and recorded with its source. Full texts have various licences, so only abstracts go into the corpus. The summaries are output of our engine, and we publish them together with the version of the model that wrote them.
3. Set the sampling frame: all papers with an abstract and a summary. From it we draw the tuning sample, the control set and the pilot sample, stratified by relevance score.
4. Describe the choice in a file in the experiment folder, in both languages: sources, legal basis for each, inclusion and exclusion criteria.
5. Add arXiv to the allowlist of sources (F2.2).

## Done when

The corpus description is published, the basis for using the abstracts is recorded with its source, and arXiv is on the allowlist of sources.
