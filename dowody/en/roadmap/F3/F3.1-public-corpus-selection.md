# F3.1. Choosing a public corpus

[← F3](../F3-first-pass.md)

> **Status: done** · as of 30 September 2026
>
> The corpus is chosen and described in [experiments/intent-vs-fact/corpus.md](../../experiments/intent-vs-fact/corpus.md) (entry of 28 September). The basis for using the abstracts, that is arXiv metadata under CC0, is recorded with its sources. arXiv is on the lab's allowlist of sources together with the terms of use of its API.

## In brief

Before we measure anything, we decide which documents we work on and whether they may be shown in public. We chose the abstracts (short summaries written by the authors) of papers from arXiv, because their metadata is available under CC0, which means anyone may reuse it. In addition there are the Polish summaries of these papers, written by our engine.

## Why

The result of an experiment can be checked only if anyone can download the same documents. Had we used private documents, or ones we may not share, the reader would have to take our word for it. The documents also have to fit the research question: many sentences about plans, hypotheses and goals next to sentences about facts, because only such documents show whether a program tells them apart. The starting point is the arXiv papers Exocortex already has: the ids are in the headers of the `wiki/papers` pages, and the engine's summaries are in the database.

## What to do

1. List the arXiv ids of all papers in `wiki/papers`, with the download date and the relevance score the engine gave them.
2. Establish the basis for use. arXiv makes paper metadata, including abstracts, available under CC0, which has to be confirmed and recorded with its source. Full texts have various licences, so only abstracts go into the corpus. The summaries are output of our engine, and we publish them together with the version of the model that wrote them.
3. Set the sampling frame, that is, the list samples are drawn from: all papers with an abstract and a summary. From it we draw the tuning sample, the control set and the pilot sample, stratified by relevance score.
4. Describe the choice in a file in the experiment folder, in both languages: sources, legal basis for each, inclusion and exclusion criteria.
5. Add arXiv to the allowlist of sources (F2.2).

## Done when

The corpus description is published, the basis for using the abstracts is recorded with its source, and arXiv is on the allowlist of sources.
