# F3.1. Choosing a public corpus

[← F3](../F3-first-pass.md)

## Why

The result of an experiment can be checked only if anyone can download the same documents. The corpus has to be public, shareable, and fit the research question: many sentences about plans, requirements and goals next to sentences about facts.

## What to do

1. Collect candidates: strategies and programmes of ministries, development plans and strategies of local governments, reports on the implementation of these documents (they put facts next to plans). For comparison, English decision documents from open projects (ADRs, RFCs) under open licences can be added.
2. Establish the basis for use of each source. Official documents and materials are as a rule not subject to copyright (Article 4 of the Polish Copyright Act), but this has to be checked for the specific source, because some documents are prepared by outside parties and published on other terms.
3. Set the size: 200 to 500 documents, with a sampling frame from which a tuning sample, a control set and a pilot sample can be drawn.
4. Describe the choice in a file in the experiment folder, in both languages: sources, legal basis for each, inclusion and exclusion criteria.
5. Add the sources to the allowlist (F2.2).

## Done when

The corpus description is published, every source has its basis for use recorded, and the allowlist contains all the chosen domains.
