## In brief

This closes the first experiment: a decision on what comes next (gate G2), and a report in Polish and English that can be understood without knowing the project. The report also describes what the method will not detect and how to recompute the results yourself from the published data.

## Why

An experiment without a report and a decision helps no one but us. The report lets an outsider understand what we tested and what came out, without reading the whole progress log, and the G2 decision, the decision point after the pilot, says whether the result justifies a next step. The task also includes recording what had to be fixed in the lab's tools along the way, so that the next experiment goes more smoothly.

## What to do

1. Compare the pilot results with the thresholds in the card. Write the G2 decision in both languages.
2. A report in both languages, a few pages at most: the question, the corpus, the method, results with confidence intervals, the error list, the judge calibration, what the method will not detect, the decision and its reasons, how to recompute the results yourself.
3. If G1 ended with a decision other than `GO`, the report describes the quick test and the reason for the decision.
4. Record in the experiment log what had to be fixed in the publishing and lab machinery along the way.

## Done when

The decision and the report are published, the report passes the language check, and a person from outside the project (for example the second expert from F6) confirms that they reproduced the report's numbers from the repository alone.
