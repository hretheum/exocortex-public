# F3.4. Hypothesis card and preregistration

[← F3](../F3-first-pass.md)

## Why

The first real card. It has to be written and frozen before the extractor is run on the corpus even once beyond the five test documents from F3.3.

## Proposed content

H1: adding a mandatory mode field to the extraction schema reduces the share of claims that describe a plan, requirement or goal as an accomplished fact, in Polish strategy documents.

H0: the difference between the variant with the mode field and without it is smaller than the threshold written in the card, or the confidence interval includes zero.

Deciding metric: the share of claims with a mode swap in a blind sample rated by a person.

Guard metrics: the share of fragments for which the model returned no valid answer (no more than 5%), the number of usable claims per document (no less than 70% of the baseline).

Baseline: the same extractor without the mode field, the same model, the same documents.

Samples: tuning 20 documents, control set 10 documents, blind sample for the pilot 120 claims. All drawn with a recorded seed, stratified by document length.

What this method will not detect: completeness (whether the most important claims were extracted), interpretation errors with a correct quote, weaknesses shared by the extracting and the rating model.

## What to do

1. Fill in the card template in both languages, starting from the proposal above. Set the threshold for the deciding metric before measuring and give the reason.
2. Review the card and set `human_validated: true`.
3. Wait for the entry in `prereg.jsonl` in the public repository.

## Done when

The card is in the repository, the preregistration entry is dated earlier than the first run on the corpus, and the F2.4 verification script confirms the checksum.
