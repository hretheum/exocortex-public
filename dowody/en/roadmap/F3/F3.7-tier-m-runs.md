# F3.7. Pilot: configuration matrix

[← F3](../F3-first-pass.md)

## Why

The quick test used one model and one way of splitting text. The pilot checks whether the effect holds for other models and settings, and what it costs.

## What to do

1. A matrix of 4 to 8 configurations: two local models from different families, the schema with and without the mode field, whole documents and fragments.
2. Run the matrix on the pilot sample (documents outside tuning and the control set), in the night window, with the queue grouping jobs by model.
3. Collect telemetry: share of fragments without a valid answer, number of claims, tokens, time, GPU use.
4. Publish the table of mechanical results (without human ratings, which come in F3.8).

## Done when

All configurations have finished runs, the telemetry table is in the repository, and runs that ended in an error are described in the log with their cause.
