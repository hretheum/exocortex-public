# F3.3. Claim extractor in the lab

[← F3](../F3-first-pass.md)

## Why

Exocortex has a claim extractor that uses a verbatim quote as grounding (a claim without a quote present in the document is rejected) and has a mode field. It has to move into the lab as clean code, without anything prepared for earlier, non-public data.

## What to do

1. Rewrite the extractor as a lab module, with code and prompts in English and support for Polish texts.
2. Keep: the quote check (a verbatim substring of the document after normalisation), a separate pass that judges whether a sentence is a claim, and removal of duplicates by embedding similarity.
3. Do not carry over the abbreviation dictionary or examples from earlier work. Build a new abbreviation dictionary from the F3.2 corpus.
4. Two variants of the output schema: without a mode field (baseline) and with a mode field (`fakt`, `plan`, `wymog`, `hipoteza`).
5. Configurations for the local models available through llama-swap.

## Done when

The extractor works on five documents from the corpus in both variants. Unit tests cover the quote check, the claim judgement and duplicate removal. The scanner finds no matches in the module, and a review of imports shows no file outside the public repository.

## Progress

- 2026-09-29: the extractor is in `exocortex/lab/extractor.py`, written anew for the lab, with nothing from earlier non-public work. It first extracts claims with a quote (the quote before the claim), then checks that the quote is a verbatim part of the document after normalisation, judges in a separate call whether each sentence is a claim, and finally removes repeats by embedding similarity. The schema variants differ by one field and one rule in the instructions. A dictionary of 70 abbreviations was built from the corpus abstracts. The lab reaches models through its own gateway, which lets through only three calls and only the models on `lab/models.yaml`; the isolation check tests it every night. Test on five papers (both variants, abstracts and Polish summaries, model qwen3.6-35b-a3b): 20 of 20 jobs with a valid answer, 190 claims extracted, 175 usable. We compute no mode-swap rate from this test, because it is not a measurement. Unit tests check the quote, the claim judgement and repeats. The scanner found nothing in the module, and the import review showed only the standard library, public packages and the repository itself.
