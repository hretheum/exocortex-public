# F3.8. Pilot: rating the blind sample and the error list

[← F3](../F3-first-pass.md)

> **Status: to do** · as of 30 September 2026
>
> The task depends on F3.7, which is "to do", so it cannot be started yet. No pilot claims have been drawn for rating yet, and there is no error list.

## In brief

A person rates 120 claims from the pilot results blind, and we compute how often each configuration presents a plan as a fact. Then we group all wrong claims into classes and describe the mechanism of each class with examples and quotes. The outcome is numbers with confidence intervals and a public error list.

## Why

The share of errors alone says how many problems there are, but not what they are or whether they can be fixed. The error list shows the mechanism of each type of error and whether it can be removed by changing the prompt, the schema or a filter, or comes from the approach itself. We derive the error classes from what actually shows up in the ratings, and set aside the list of errors we expected, so that expectations do not distort the description.

## What to do

1. Draw 120 claims from the F3.7 results (with the F3.5 tool) and rate them blind.
2. Compute the share of mode swaps for each configuration, with confidence intervals.
3. Go through all wrong claims and group them into classes. For each class: a description of the mechanism, two or three examples with quotes, the count, and whether the error is visible when reading the claim and quote pair.
4. For each class, record whether it can be removed by changing the prompt, the schema or a filter, or whether it comes from the approach itself.
5. The error list in both languages in the experiment folder.

## Done when

Ratings for 120 claims are in the table, the error list is published, and the numbers in it can be recomputed from the data in `dowody/data/` with the F2.8 script.
