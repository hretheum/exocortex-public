# F3.9. Pilot: calibrating the automatic judge

[← F3](../F3-first-pass.md)

## Why

Human rating does not scale to the whole corpus. A model from a different family than the extractor can serve as a screen, but only once we know how many errors it catches and how many false alarms it raises.

## What to do

1. Choose a judge model from a different family than the extracting models.
2. Run it on the same 120 claims with a prompt aimed at finding errors.
3. Compare with the human ratings: sensitivity for each error class from F3.8, and the share of correct claims marked as wrong.
4. Record the conclusion: can the judge decide, can it only route claims to review, or is it of no use.

## Done when

The calibration results in both languages are in the experiment folder, with numbers and a conclusion on acceptable use of the judge.
