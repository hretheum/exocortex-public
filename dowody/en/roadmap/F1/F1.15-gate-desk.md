# F1.15. The review desk: a web service and a Quadlet unit

[← F1](../F1-public-repo.md)

## Why

Reviewing held items in Obsidian is long and needs a job started by hand on the server. An ordinary page with two actions per finding turns it into a single pass through the queue.

## What to do

1. Write `tools/gate/desk.py`: a service on the standard library, like simcheck, listening on 127.0.0.1 on port 8100, with screens for the queue, the focus mode, the rules and the history.
2. Focus mode: the public paragraph and the three nearest protected ones, the actions "keep" (key `1`) and "to edit" (key `2`), the card collapsing after a decision and the next one opening, `Backspace` undoing, `↓` skipping, a header with progress.
3. The bulk actions "keep the whole experiment" and "keep the whole folder" with a summary and a confirmation, disabled for literal findings, and an optional standing rule with a reason and an expiry.
4. The actions "take the note out" and "take the folder out" next to every protected paragraph, with a reason, revoking and the hard list of forbidden paths.
5. Authentication with the token from the secret `gate_desk_token`, a CSRF token, no external resources, no logging of content, the header `Cache-Control: no-store`.
6. Add `exocortex-gate-desk.container` to `deploy/gate/quadlet/`, installation notes and tests with a synthetic index, including a keyboard pass through the queue in a headless browser.
7. After a trial on the real queue, remove the `review` and `approve` jobs and the review page in Obsidian.

## Done when

The owner goes through a queue of 60 findings in the desk, bulk approval of an experiment works, an excluded note disappears from findings, a request without the token gets 401, and the page makes no request outside its own address.
