# F1.14. Quarantine database, approvals and the exclusion list

[← F1](../F1-public-repo.md)

## Why

The desk needs a durable record of what was held, what was decided and what was taken out of protection. The record should exist before the interface, so that it can be tested and used from the command line.

## What to do

1. Add `tools/publisher/quarantine.py`: an SQLite database on the state volume with units, findings (rule, path, paragraph hash, score), states, the source hash and a decision trail, without text.
2. Extend the simcheck `/check` response with paragraphs and scores, and make the publisher record findings on every run without duplicates.
3. Move approved paragraph hashes to the state volume and read decisions on the next run: a unit with no open findings and no findings marked "to edit" is released, and a change in the source makes old findings outdated.
4. Add a list of sources taken out of protection (paths or engine database identifiers, a reason, an expiry) read by simcheck on every check and by the index build, and a hard list of paths that cannot be excluded.
5. Add standing "keep" rules with a reason and an expiry, never for literal findings.
6. Provide the command line commands `list`, `keep`, `edit`, `exclude` and `revoke` as an emergency path, and report the numbers of open findings and active exclusions in the notification.

## Done when

Two runs with the same content give one entry, a changed paragraph makes the old one outdated, an approval from the command line releases the unit on the next run, and an excluded note disappears from check results at once.
