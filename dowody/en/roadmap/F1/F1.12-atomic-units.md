# F1.12. Units of publication and skipping whole experiments

[← F1](../F1-public-repo.md)

## Why

Holding one file of an experiment leaves half of it in the repository. An experiment has to go out as a whole or not at all.

## What to do

1. In `tools/publisher/core.py`, group files into units: an experiment is `experiments/<slug>/**` in both languages, `data/<slug>/**` and the registry lines with that slug.
2. If any file of a unit fails the checks, copy none of them, keep the public version and record the reason for every path. The other units go out in the same commit.
3. Build the public version of `prereg.jsonl` from lines: append at the end only the lines of units that passed, and check the rule "it only grows" as the presence of all published lines in the source, unchanged.
4. Hold an incomplete unit (the other language missing, or the card missing when there is a registry line) with the reason `incomplete`, and remove an experiment from the source as a whole.
5. Write a scenario test: the data pass, the card is held, so nothing of that experiment goes out, while another experiment goes out in the same commit.

## Done when

The scenario test passes, the registry after two runs with different units has all lines in the order of publication, and the lab site builds from every intermediate state.
