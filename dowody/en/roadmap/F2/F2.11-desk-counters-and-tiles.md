# F2.11. Desk: counters and tiles instead of tables

[← F2](../F2-lab.md)

## Why

The owner's desk does not show how much waits for a decision until each screen is opened. Wide tables, for example the metrics table of a hypothesis card, do not fit the screen: the last columns fall off the edge, and in narrow columns the text breaks after every word. The lab site has the same problem.

## What to do

1. Counters next to the menu entries "Queue" and "Ocena na ślepo": how many units wait for a decision and how many claims wait for a rating. The server counts them with one light request, the page refreshes them after every action and when the tab comes back, and a counter disappears at zero.
2. On the rating screen, rated items move into a folded list "Ocenione", from which they can be opened and the rating changed. The list of samples shows separately how many items are rated and how many wait.
3. One rule for tables on the desk and on the lab site: a table with at most three short columns stays a table, and a wider one becomes a list of tiles. One row is one tile, the first column is its title, and the others are "heading: value" pairs in a grid that folds into one column on a phone. Column headings stay available to screen readers.

## Done when

The logic tests (counters, the split into waiting and rated, the choice of table or tiles) pass, browser screenshots at 1280 and 400 pixels wide show the metrics table of the "Intent or fact" card with no column cut off, and the counters work on the desk on the server.

## Progress

- 2026-09-30: the counters, the "Ocenione" list and the tiles are on the desk and on the lab site. Before the change the metrics table on the site was 799 pixels wide in a 724 pixel box (328 on a phone), so the last column was cut off. After the change nothing sticks out of the screen at 1280 or at 400 pixels, in the dark theme too.
