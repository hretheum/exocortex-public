# F2.10. Blind rating in the owner's interface

[← F2](../F2-lab.md)

## Why

The rating page in Obsidian (F3.5) works, but with more than a hundred claims it is awkward and easy to get wrong. Every experiment with human rating needs fast rating: the quick test and the pilot of the first experiment (F3.6, F3.8), the gold answer set for retrieval (F5.5) and the later ones.

## What to do

1. A rating screen in the owner's interface: one claim at a time, its quote and the passage around the quote, without the configuration name, in random order. Buttons for the categories (correct, mode swap, garbled number or name, other error), a comment field, a jump to the next item after rating, keyboard shortcuts and resuming after a break.
2. Ratings go to the `exp_judgments` table through the same validation code as the import from the page, including the repeated items that check the rater's agreement with themselves.
3. A progress view (how many rated, how many left), without partial results, so they cannot influence later ratings.
4. The page in Obsidian stays as a fallback.

## Done when

On the toy experiment all items are rated in the interface, and the results and confidence intervals are the same as when loaded from the page. The rater sees neither configurations nor partial results.

## Progress

- 2026-09-30: the "Blind rating" screen works in the owner's interface. On the toy experiment all items (4 claims and 2 repeats, test ratings by the agent) were rated in the interface, and the results and confidence intervals are identical to loading the same ratings from a page filled in by hand. Neither the screen nor the server's answers contain configurations or partial results.
