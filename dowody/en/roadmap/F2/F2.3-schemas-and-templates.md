# F2.3. Header schemas and final templates

[← F2](../F2-lab.md)

## Why

The processors need to be able to rely on the structure of files. A hypothesis card with a typo in a field name must not quietly disappear or be read differently.

## What to do

1. JSON Schema definitions for headers: hypothesis card, experiment note, gate decision, roadmap task file.
2. A validator (code in English) run in CI, in the publisher and in the processors.
3. Final templates in `dowody/pl/templates/` and `dowody/en/templates/`, matching the schemas. The working templates in that folder are the starting point.
4. Experiment folder layout: `dowody/pl/experiments/<slug>/` and `dowody/en/experiments/<slug>/`, each with `hypothesis.md`, `run-*.md` notes and `gate-*.md` decisions.

## Done when

The templates pass validation, and three prepared bad files (missing field, wrong value, unknown field) are rejected with a readable message.
